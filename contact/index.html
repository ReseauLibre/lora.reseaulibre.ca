    Contact¶

    Chat¶

    We talk on the mesh! In little Italy, there are at least some messages every week.

    We have real time chat in the #reseaulibre:matrix.org Matrix room. We're organizing those many rooms in a wider #mesh-quebec:matrix.org space, as an alternative to the Telegram chat1.

    Email¶

    We're currently reusing the old Réseau Libre mailing lists:

    • nodes - discussion list (nodes@reseaulibre.ca)
    • nouvelles - announcements, low-traffic

    Meetups¶

    Every month, Foulab, the Montreal hacker space, holds a mesh night. It is located at Suite 33B, 999 du Collège, Montréal, Québec, Canada, H4C 2S3 (Google maps link), just a few minutes away from Place-Saint-Henri metro station.

    Check the News pages to confirm the specific date each month, but it is typically on the first Wednesday of the month.

    Issues¶

    If you find an issue with this website, or fail to use all the above options, file an issue.

    1. We do not endorse the Telegram channel for various reasons, but more importantly the author of this site (Anarcat) refuses to join because screw proprietary software. ↩
